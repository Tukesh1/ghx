# Code of conduct

ghx follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer via [GitHub](https://github.com/Tukesh1). Reports
are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break these rules.
